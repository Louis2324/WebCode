# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in WebCode welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcoming questions from beginners.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on the work and its impact rather than on individuals.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or imagery, unwanted contact, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily or permanently restrict participation, or take any other action they consider appropriate to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through the project owner’s [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Louis2324). For security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and as confidentially as possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project or its community.
